Community-Based Blood Donor Management and Emergency Matching System

Welcome! The Community-Based Blood Donor Management and Emergency Matching System is a web-based platform designed to connect volunteer blood donors, hospitals, barangay health workers, and the City Health Office for faster emergency blood donor matching and coordination.
